Tarrow is a personal AI agent that knows your life and does the work, with an interface inspired by developer terminals.

IT STARTS WITH A CONVERSATION
A short interview about your work, money, goals, people and what's getting in your way. Tarrow keeps a living "life file" on you so every answer fits your situation. You can read and edit everything it remembers.

THEN IT GETS TO WORK
Ask for anything, like "find me three events worth going to this week" or "research moving to a new neighborhood and make me a plan." Tarrow searches the web, reads pages, checks maps, compares options and verifies details. For big jobs it runs several agents in parallel. You watch every step live in the transcript.

IT THINKS AS LONG AS THE TASK NEEDS
• Fast: quick answers in seconds
• Standard: research and actions in a few minutes
• Deep: parallel agents and a thorough plan
• Marathon: exhaustive research with a full report saved to your Library

IT TAKES ACTION
Goals and tasks, calendar events you add in one tap, drafted messages and emails for you to send, and routines that run in the background, always with your approval.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
View, edit or delete anything Tarrow remembers, or delete your account at any time.
