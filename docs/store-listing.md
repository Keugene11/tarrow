## Description

Tarrow is a personal AI agent that actually knows your life, and does the work.

**It starts with a conversation.** A 5-minute interview about your work, money, goals, people and what's getting in your way. Tarrow keeps a living "life file" on you, so every answer fits your situation. You can read and edit everything it remembers.

**Then it gets to work.** Ask for anything, from "find me three events worth going to this week" to "research moving to a new neighborhood and make me a plan." Tarrow searches the web, reads pages, checks Google Maps, compares options and verifies details. For big jobs it spins up several agents working in parallel. You see every step live, like watching someone work.

**It thinks as long as the task needs:**
- **Fast:** quick advice in seconds
- **Standard:** research and actions in a few minutes
- **Deep:** parallel agents and a thorough plan
- **Marathon:** exhaustive research with a full report saved to your Library

**It takes action.** Tarrow adds goals and tasks, creates calendar events you can add in one tap, drafts messages and emails for you to send, and runs routines in the background (like finding the best events every Monday), always with your approval.

**It's proactive.** Your home screen fills with personalized ideas for what Tarrow can do next to move your life forward.

**Private by design.** Your memories are yours: view, edit or delete anything, or delete your account at any time.
